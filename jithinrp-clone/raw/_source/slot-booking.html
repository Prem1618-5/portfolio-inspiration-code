Context & Problem

Choosing a slot was harder than it needed to be.

Users could find a venue and reach the slot selection screen, but making the final decision wasn't always straightforward. They had to figure out when their session would end, how long they were booking, what they would pay, and whether they were getting the best available option.

The Insight - Users wanted confidence, not more options

The opportunity wasn't to add more information. It was to make the information already available easier to understand at the moment of decision.

Exploring Directions - We explored different ways to make comparison easier

We tested multiple approaches before landing on a solution that made time and duration immediately understandable.

The Breakthrough - Make time immediately understandable

The dual pointer timeline changed how users understood their session. Start time, duration and end time became one connected interaction.

Designing for Confidence - Making every decision around the slot clearer

Once the core time selection was simplified, we looked at the decisions around it. Date, price, rewards and booking context were redesigned to help users move forward without second guessing.

01

Choose

Date + Slot Selection

Plan your game with clarity. See the real-time availability and pick the exact time that works for you.

02

Discover

We surfaced venue specific offers at the moment users were deciding, making promotions easier to discover without interrupting the booking flow.

03

Recover

Don't let an unavailable slot end the journey

When a user's preferred slot wasn't available, we didn't treat it as the end of the journey. We surfaced nearby venues with availability for the same time.

04

Commit

Make the final booking feel safe

We introduced insurance during checkout, giving users an additional layer of protection while creating a relevant opportunity to opt in at the moment of purchase.

The Outcome - More users completed the booking they already intended to make

The redesigned experience improved conversion and helped more users move from selecting a slot to completing payment.

After Launch - The redesign didn't stop at launch

Launch validated the core experience, but real usage uncovered behaviours we hadn't anticipated. Those became the next opportunities to improve the journey.

Takeaway - We didn't make booking more complicated

We removed the uncertainty that was stopping people from finishing it.
