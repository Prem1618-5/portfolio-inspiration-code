Side project · Post office mornings

Australia Post Parcel Sorter

One scan tells you where a parcel goes: Town, Country, PO Box or Counter. I built it while working mornings as a store assistant, alongside the two owners.

The 7 AM van

I started at 5:30. Parcels arrive in crates, and each one has to be sorted to Town, Country, PO Box or the counter before the delivery van collects at 7 sharp. We were always late: 30+ parcels a day missed the van and waited until the next morning.

The rule was easy. The exceptions weren't.

Streets, drives and places go to Town; roads and lanes go to Country. But some roads go to Town, some streets go to Country, and some customers ask to collect from the counter. All of that lived in one printed sheet, so every parcel meant a lookup. In my first days I checked every single one against it.

More hands, or fewer lookups?

On my first two days I talked to the owners. Their plan was to hire two more people. But the slow part wasn't carrying parcels; it was checking the sheet. More people would just mean more people looking things up.

One scan, one answer

I loaded the sheet into a simple scanner app. Scan a label and the screen answers in colour and in words: Town, Country, PO Box or Counter, exceptions included. No reading the sheet, no remembering which roads break the rule.

The counter, too

Held parcels piled up at the counter, often 20+, and finding one meant searching while a queue grew. With the same app, a quick check showed whether a customer's parcel was there, so we could pick it and hand it over.

What changed

Sorting got faster and far easier. I was finishing before the owners, who had done it for years, and pickups went from searching a pile to a quick look.

iPhone game · Coming soon to the App Store

UNDO

A calm puzzle about loosening knots. Pull a strand and feel it give; tilt the phone and the loose loops fall that way.

Pull, tilt, untangle

Each puzzle is a glossy 3D knot. You pull strands one at a time and tilt the phone to let loose loops slide, until the tangle opens into clean rings.

A quiet invitation

You choose your rhythm. With your permission, UNDO sends one quiet notification at the times you pick: nothing during quiet hours, and nothing if you skip a day.

Keep what you solve

Solved knots collect in a gallery, with badges and a profile that grow as you go.

iPhone & iPad game · SwiftUI + SpriteKit

Stickskate

An endless stickman skateboarding game drawn in ink on paper. Skate as far as you can through a built skate-park course.

Simple to play

Swipe up or tap to jump, swipe down to duck under bars. You can pick it up in seconds.

Tricks in slow motion

When you catch air, time slows and the camera moves in close. Swipe for a backflip, kickflip, 360 shove-it or grab, and chain them in one jump for combo coins. Land too early and you bail.

A real skate park

Kickers, decks, grind rails, ledges, roofs, stairs, loop barrels and rooftop gaps, across five places that blend into each other: city, jungle, desert, beach and snow. It gets faster every 500 m, up to 5,000 m.

Collect and compete

Badges for stunts and big runs, your top 10 plus Game Center leaderboards, boosters, and custom tracks you can share as a challenge.
